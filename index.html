The Secrets of Parchin Kari (Marble Inlay)

When Emperor Shah Jahan commissioned the Taj Mahal in 1632, master lapidaries perfected Parchin Kari (also known as Pietra Dura) — the intricate art of hand-chiseling white Makrana marble and seamlessly inlaying cut facets of precious and semi-precious gemstones.

In a single floral bud measuring barely an inch, master artisans fit up to 35 individual facets of Lapis Lazuli, Malachite, Jasper, Cornelian, and Onyx with invisible joints.

Today in Agra, the direct descendants of the royal Mughal stone-carvers still preserve this unbroken guild tradition. On our private tours, you can observe master craftsmen shaping gems on traditional bow-lathes — with zero commercial purchase pressure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you prepare for your journey to the City of the Taj Mahal.

Is the Taj Mahal open every day of the week? +
The Taj Mahal opens 30 minutes before sunrise and closes 30 minutes before sunset every day EXCEPT FRIDAYS. On Fridays, the monument is strictly closed for general visitors. However, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri, Itimad-ud-Daulah, and Mehtab Bagh remain open on Fridays, allowing for alternative full-day itineraries.
What are the official entry ticket fees in 2026? +
Official Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) rates: Foreign Tourists pay ₹1,100 standard entry + ₹200 for main mausoleum dome access (total ₹1,300 / approx $16 USD). Indian citizens pay ₹50 + ₹200 dome access (total ₹250). Children under 15 enter free. All TajMahal.City private tours include fast-track advance online ticket procurement so you bypass queue ticket windows completely.
What items are strictly prohibited inside the monument? +
Strict airport-style security scans operate at all gates. Prohibited items include: tripods, camera gimbals/stabilizers, drones, electronic chargers/cables, knives, lighters, cigarettes, and chewing gum. Safe cloakrooms/lockers are available at both East and West gates to securely store unauthorized items before entry.
How does the same-day Delhi to Agra tour work? +
Our chauffeur picks you up from your hotel or Delhi Airport (DEL) in a sanitized AC vehicle via the 6-lane Yamuna Expressway (approx 3 hours). In Agra, your private certified guide joins you for skip-the-line sightseeing of the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort, followed by lunch and a smooth return to Delhi by evening.
